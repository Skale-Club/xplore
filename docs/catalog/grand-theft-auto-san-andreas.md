# Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas — original PS2 catalog notes

## Edition and progression

The selected edition is the original 2004 PlayStation 2 game, not the mobile-based PS3 remaster, the later PS4 conversion or the Definitive Edition. No native PS2 trophies are invented.

The final source has 17 categories, 173 bilingual tasks (94 story / 69 side / ten aggregate goals), 22 optional hidden Details and no trophies. The 22 additional tournament events have individual searchable titles. Import lists and location collections remain explicitly aggregate goals, without pretending to provide a per-pickup location guide.

The route follows Los Santos, the countryside, San Fierro, the desert, Las Venturas and the return to Los Santos through End of the Line. Zero's RC shop, driving school/Wang Cars and the casino heist are optional for the ending but matter to full completion. Flight school is required for story progression. Mission totals vary with introductory and Catalina transition cutscenes; checklist preparation milestones must not be described as extra missions.

References: [original regional route](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtasa/missions.html), [mission dependency index](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-san-andreas/missions/).

## Independent mechanics review

The original [End of the Line route](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtasa/missions6_2.html) confirms the 35% Los Santos territory gate. Capturing every territory is a separate optional challenge. The finale's building escape and subsequent pursuit require different objectives; optional Firefighter rewards can help without becoming extra mandatory story tasks.

[Supply Lines version notes](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto%3A_San_Andreas/Supply_Lines...) distinguish early PS2 fuel depletion while gliding from revised editions' throttle-saving advice. Guidance must retain that difference and include the return to Zero's roof.

Old Amphibious Assault FAQs disagree about minimum lung capacity. The [mission's script excerpt](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Amphibious_Assault) checks a value of 50 on the thousand-point stat scale, rather than the 20% or 50% requirements claimed by some guides. Practical guidance should train by diving and surfacing safely, then return during the mission's night window when the game accepts the stat. Do not present an exaggerated percentage as mandatory.

The [original Las Venturas walkthrough](https://www.gtasanandreas.net/walkthrough/lasventuras.php) independently verifies Freefall's aerial interception, cabin fight, pilot and return landing, plus Madd Dogg's truck alignment and hospital drive. [RonHiler's original PS2 route](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/914983-grand-theft-auto-san-andreas/faqs/38136) checks Black Project's alternate vent entry and disabling the internal SAM controls before escape. Exact searchlight counts differ among old guides and are not necessary to promise a successful route.

Root corrected the casino-heist wording from restoring power to destroying the backup generators when instructed, using the [original heist route](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtasa/missions5_2.html). Stowaway explicitly says to plant the charge and jump through the rear ramp, avoiding an invented extra manual-detonation requirement. English and Portuguese were updated together without changing keys.

## Ending versus 100%

[Original completion checklist](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtasa/100.html): taxi 50 cumulative, Pimping level 10, Paramedic/Firefighter/Vigilante level 12 and Freight Train level 2. Full completion includes four collectible sets (100 tags, 50 snapshots, 50 horseshoes and 50 oysters), bronze across all four schools, three gym fighting styles, three import lists, courier work, quarry/trucking/valet jobs, stadiums and other required challenges. Safehouse and asset purchases must also be checked.

The 70 unique jumps, Burglary reward, girlfriends, triathlons, maximum skills, all-gold schools and total territory ownership are optional extras rather than additional native 100% requirements.

[Tournament routing](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtasa/race.html) lists 25 events; three are already credited through story races, leaving 22 additional events. Land events need first place; the six airport courses need completion. A checklist may list all events for navigation, but must not require repeating the credited story races. [Original odd-job index](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtasa/omissions.html) verifies the activity groups.
