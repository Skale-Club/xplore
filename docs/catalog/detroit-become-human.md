## Required early preparation

All profiles deliberately find the attic suspect in Partners so The Interrogation exists. The A–F baseline keeps Kara/Alice alive in Stormy Night, uses the squat for a predictable On the Run chase, gives up Connor's highway pursuit, saves Hank at The Nest, spares the Tracis/Chloe and maintains a friendly Hank. Russian Roulette supplies Cole's photograph; Sumo is the dog's name. Software instability and Hank friendship are different variables, not interchangeable meters.

The peaceful broadcast baseline uses a ruse, spares the fleeing employee, saves wounded Simon and leaves him alive on the roof. Connor does not find Simon there; he investigates the kitchen android, recovers his regulator in time and uses RUSH DEVIANT to preserve the kitchen body as evidence without losing Connor/Hank. Later he reactivates that body using the indicated compatible component, studies the broadcast, copies Markus's voice and obtains Jericho's location. Daniel's body alone does not supply it. Sparing Chloe preserves this evidence-room route; obtaining Kamski's direct key would skip it.

A/D/E need public support for the successful peaceful singing conclusion, maintained by the calm broadcast, peaceful Capitol Park, spared officers and dispersing/running at Freedom March. B/C/F can use the same early history and choose Revolution later. A–E need enough instability to choose deviance; a locked final option can require an earlier saved replay, not only repeating the last conversation. A–F preserve Markus's leadership and rescue Josh/North during Crossroads. Kara helps Luther before the later outdoor escape/capture decision.

G uses Experienced, keeps Kara obeying Todd, and later selects ATTACK, loses and explicitly selects SHOOT for Markus's suicide. Do not substitute waiting/DON'T SHOOT, where John/Simon may rescue him. Connor does not obtain Kamski's key and lets Last Chance's timer expire without locating Jericho, permanently ending his story before Crossroads. That is different from a replaceable earlier Connor death.

## Final-stage audit

The peaceful route refuses Perkins's deal, wins the defense and sings with supportive opinion. The violent route uses cover and tactical commands; friendly opinion does not replace winning the assault. Independent chapter references corrected a misleading legacy comment: at the final three-group machine gun, the front-right group charges and both others, including North, provide covering fire; launch while the weapon sweeps left and shoot the drone afterward. At the earlier two-group barrier, the nearer group charges and the farther one covers. B/C finish by taking the center; F instead selects Connor and wins the final duel. Crossroads's earlier gun struggle is played as Markus, not a character-selection menu: winning it preserves Markus and allows a replacement machine Connor to return.

CyberLife paths redirect the elevator to -49, save Hank from the duplicate, answer Sumo/Cole and resume conversion. A/B/D/E activate the emergency exit in Amanda's snowy garden; C deliberately does not. The latter kills Markus at the final rally, after camp liberation, and can award Mission Complete even though Connor had become deviant. That trophy description must not falsely restrict it to the machine branch or an earlier Crossroads kill.

Kara's bus paths rescue Luther/Jerry on the streets, use the safe dialogue through the short checkpoint, keep the dropped tickets and avoid terminal thermal patrols. A uses no sacrifice while Markus demonstrates; E sacrifices Kara with Luther alive; F sacrifices Jerry because Markus is fighting. D returns the tickets, finds Adam/Rose, chooses Dive under coastguard fire, lightens the boat and reaches shore with Kara/Alice unwounded. Luther's river death is unavoidable on that chosen path. B/C surrender at Crossroads, keep Alice calm and the family alive in the recall-center queues, then depend on Markus actually liberating it; they do not also require a bus or river crossing.

## Replay, trophies and magazines

The shared opening checkpoint explains the full saved-replay contract. From the earliest changed prerequisite, select SAVE and play all intervening chapters for every character in order. Jumping directly to a later chapter loads its previously recorded history; it does not propagate an isolated earlier edit. A new saved story is the safe fallback if the current history is unknown. The app checkpoint only navigates the guide and cannot modify the console. Shared checks are guide coverage across replays, not separate playthrough save slots or proof that every checked outcome belongs to the current timeline.

The [native PS4 list](https://www.exophase.com/game/detroit-become-human-psn/trophies/) and [tier/criteria reference](https://www.powerpyx.com/detroit-become-human-trophy-guide-roadmap/) confirm **49 trophies: forty bronze, five silver, three gold and one platinum**. All native title/tier pairs were independently compared. Gold: This Is My Story, These Are Our Stories, Survivors. Silver: Undefeated, Bookworm, Partners, Happy Family, I'll Be Back. No DLC/online trophy group is invented.

I'LL BE BACK has its own detailed guide, deliberately separate from the friendly-Hank profiles: eight required pre-finale deaths in one saved continuous run, through Hostage, Interrogation, On the Run, Nest, Bridge, Public Enemy, Last Chance and Crossroads. It preserves the interview/pursuit, makes Hank hostile, kills the Tracis for later evidence, avoids Kamski's location shortcut, makes Gavin suspicious and solves the archive before losing the confrontation. Permanent decommission is not a replaceable death. Continue through Night of the Soul into Battle for Detroit for the award. The two possible later finale deaths are not additional required deaths. [Dedicated death-run reference](https://www.powerpyx.com/detroit-become-human-ill-be-back-trophy-guide-all-connor-deaths/).

BOOKWORM uses forty-six collectible covers with branch-dependent reports and multiple cover topics on some magazines. It cannot be completed by pretending all variants appear in one timeline. The one aggregate goal and help guide explicitly do not enumerate every location. [Magazine reference](https://www.powerpyx.com/detroit-become-human-magazine-locations/). UNDEFEATED is separate from the intentionally lost fights of death-run/G; optional cleanup checks may accumulate across replays. KNOW YOUR PARTNER's desk clues and headphones are distinct from Cole's photograph in Russian Roulette.
