# Grand Theft Auto Advance — original GBA catalog notes

## Edition and scope

This is the original 2004 Game Boy Advance game. The source has 11 categories, 75 bilingual tasks (43 story: 41 named missions plus two preparation steps; 27 side; five aggregate goals), 12 optional hidden Details and no native trophies. No untracked stunt-jump checklist is invented.

The overall mission order is linear through Portland, Staunton Island and Shoreside Vale. Individual jobs can still offer local choices; this does not create alternate campaigns to register. All 18 numbered races are individual tasks: six per island, progressing through the island sequences. Large package/rampage location sets remain clearly aggregate goals.

Sources: [original main route](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/missions.html), [Portland](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/missions1.html), [Staunton](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/missions2.html), [Shoreside](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/missions3.html), [race sequences](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/race.html).

## Independent GBA mechanics audit

The [original service guide](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/r3missions.html) differs from GTA III: the ambulance collects every patient in a level before returning, has room for that full group and resumes after cleared levels on another attempt. Do not prescribe a three-patient capacity or twelve consecutive levels. Taxi requires 100 cumulative fares; the Borgnine reward at 50 is not the 100% condition. Firefighter and Vigilante require 20 per island. The service toggle uses L+R, with R first to avoid exiting through L alone.

[Demolition Football](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/demolition-football.html) follows the Staunton races and requires four successful distance stages. The Banshee burns after landing, so exit promptly. [Native completion](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/100.html) also includes 100 hidden packages and 21 rampages; jump tricks are not a separate native collection requirement. Conflicting published 64/65-flag totals are not repeated as a promised checklist count.

Root reviewed all extended guidance and independently checked [Grand Opening, Pocket Rocket and Political Will](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/missions1-3.html), [vehicle preservation](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/missions1-2.html), [Asuka's rescue/driver interrogation](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/missions2-3.html), [Assault Joint](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/missions3-4.html) and [Freedom Flies](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtaad/missions3-5.html). Two selective root additions explain preserving the mission Rocket Launcher and using fists on the politician, then clearing the wanted level before returning. The finale starts automatically after Assault Joint and offers a completed-state save after its five-minute escape.
