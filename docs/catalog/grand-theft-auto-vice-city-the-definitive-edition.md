# Grand Theft Auto: Vice City – The Definitive Edition — PS5 catalog notes

## Separate remaster and route

This is a new native PS5 remaster registration, separate from the original PS2 entry. [PlayStation's official Trilogy description](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10003543/) identifies the remastered campaign. The source preserves the original entry's 126 task keys and relative order without changing that entry. It contains eighteen categories, 138 bilingual tasks (38 story, 91 side and nine aggregate goals), 25 optional hidden Details and 34 authentic PS5 trophies.

The six purchased income-asset ending gate, including Print Works, remains distinct from full native completion. Sunshine Autos' first delivery list qualifies its story income; all four lists and the additional races remain full-completion work. The [DE completion reference](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-vice-city-definitive-100-completion-guide-checklist/) supports the complete asset, service, race and collection scope. The guide does not use an old repeated-Boatyard percentage exploit to substitute for unfinished goals.

## Native trophies and changed guidance

The [native PS5 list](https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vice-city-the-definitive-edition-psn-2/trophies/) has nineteen bronze, nine silver, five gold and one platinum, all offline. [The DE roadmap](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-vice-city-definitive-edition-trophy-guide-roadmap/) identifies four missable methods: Four Iron with a Golf Club, Demolition Man's blade eliminations, Rub Out's M4 finish and G-Spotlight without rooftop falls. Keep pre-mission manual saves; retrying a failed mission is not a completed-mission replay menu. Advanced methods are only in hidden Details.

Root independently checked the DE timing distinction: Demolition Man gives 7:30 after the first bomb pickup; Checkpoint Charlie's first course gives 3:00 for 26 checkpoints. Do not import the original PS2 7:00/2:30 text. [Demolition reference](https://www.igrandtheftauto.com/gtavc/guides/demolition-man), [DE Boatyard reference](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-vice-city-definitive-100-completion-guide-checklist/). Original PS2 Cherry Popper save reports are removed from remaster guidance without claiming every historical bug is proven fixed in the current patch.

Take the Cannoli requires the actual Godfather rank in the updated DE. Its point target is 100,000, rather than the old 1,000,000. [Updated DE trophy roadmap](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-vice-city-definitive-edition-trophy-guide-roadmap/). The optional guidance retains a cash buffer and checks the actual Stats label; [this edition-aware guide](https://gameskeys.net/take-the-cannoli-trophy-guide-gta-vice-city/) reports the additional $10 million context. Raw cash is not presented as equivalent to criminal rank, and no old money/percentage exploit is guaranteed to work in every patch.
