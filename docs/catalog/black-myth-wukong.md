## Scope

The original PS5 campaign is covered by 19 categories and 141 bilingual steps: 97 story, 33 selected side-content goals and 11 aggregate collection goals. Side goals include chapter secrets, missable encounters, the horse/old-man threads and the hidden finale. This is a practical route, not an individual location list for every collectible. Sixteen longer explanations are optional spoilers behind Details.

The native PS5 trophy list has 36 entries: 22 bronze, nine silver, four gold and one platinum. PC/Xbox have a different 81-achievement list and are not substituted. Unlock descriptions use original, concise condition text instead of copying the game's poems. New Game+ requirements are distinguished from first-cycle cleanup.
