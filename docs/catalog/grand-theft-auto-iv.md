# Grand Theft Auto IV — PS3 catalog notes

## Edition and route

This entry is the original 2008 PlayStation 3 game, without either story expansion or the Complete Edition bundle. It contains 19 categories, 143 bilingual tasks (84 story, 55 side, four aggregate collection/completion goals), 19 optional hidden Details and 51 native base-game trophies.

Only one ending is needed. Three shared stages represent Deal or Revenge, rather than requiring six mutually exclusive missions on one save. No. 1 and the Dwayne/Playboy conflict remain searchable optional story strands required for full completion. App task counts are editorial checkpoints, not a claim about every version's native mission counter. References: [original completion checklist](https://media.gtanet.com/gta4/files/100-Percent-Checklist.pdf), [mission routes](https://www.gtabase.com/grand-theft-auto-iv/missions/), [No. 1 can follow the ending](https://www.gtabase.com/grand-theft-auto-iv/missions/no-1), [firsthand contemporary walkthrough](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/grand-theft-auto-iv-walkthrough/1100-6191591/).

## Native completion and separate trophies

Native single-player completion includes ten Jacob deliveries, ten Brucie email exports, thirty Stevie text requests, thirty Most Wanted targets, twenty current-crime dispatches, nine Fixer contracts, nine distinct Brucie races, the required friendship activities/abilities, games, ten full required random-character strands, 200 pigeons and fifty jumps. Twenty race wins, helicopter tours, bridge flights and multiplayer are separate trophy goals. Random-character trophy encounters are not equivalent to finishing every follow-up for native completion; Jeff's first meeting has a trophy role but his strand is excluded from native 100%.

Root independently checked [the original completion breakdown](https://gta.rdy.jp/gta4/100.html) and [GTA Base's checklist](https://www.gtabase.com/grand-theft-auto-iv/gta-4-100-completion-guide-checklist). Friendship tasks use the actual offered phone ability instead of conflicting percentage thresholds in guides. Optional Details explain the thirty-hour story trophy, Roman's potentially missable taxi ability, Dwayne's email, the final branches and difficult missions. They remain hidden until explicitly opened.

[Sony's original announcement](https://blog.playstation.com/2008/10/24/grand-theft-auto-iv-ps-3-trophies-this-monday/) confirms 51 base PS3 trophies and the trophy-enabling update. The base distribution is 42 bronze, five silver, three gold and one platinum. The ten multiplayer trophies are labeled explicitly. [The full PS3 list](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-iv/trophies/) has 66 total: base 51, Lost and Damned five bronze and Ballad of Gay Tony ten bronze. DLC is registered separately and is not required for the base platinum.

[Liberty City (5)](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-iv/trophy/3176-liberty-city-5-.html) checks applicable surviving friends; a story death does not itself force a restart. [No More Strangers](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-iv/trophy/3177-No-More-Strangers.html) is not blocked by killing Ivan, Cherise or Clarence in their story encounters. Their conditional availability is not presented as a mandatory all-survive route.

The optional co-op task names all three missions without hard-coding conflicting target-time figures from secondary references. [Rockstar's PS3 update note](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/ctloXHXes1T5jwSwy3K0I/grand-theft-auto-iv-title-update-1-07-ps3-only) documents replacement of GameSpy matchmaking, but does not establish current server availability. No such availability claim is made.
