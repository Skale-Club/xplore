# The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time — Nintendo 64 catalog notes

## Edition boundary

This is the original 1998 Nintendo 64 campaign. It remains a separate slug from the existing Switch 2 remake. Use original N64 dungeon layouts and mechanics, not the 3DS, Master Quest or 2026 remake route. The campaign covers child and adult Link, the three Spiritual Stones, five adult temples, their required side areas and the final castle/boss route. Optional trades, upgrades and collection goals are separate from main story progression. Nintendo 64 has no invented native PlayStation trophy list.

## Cover artwork

Final artwork review located an official 500 × 500 square in Nintendo's European catalog, tied to the exact Nintendo 64 product URL. This replaces the initially chosen full 360 × 254 history-page box-art fallback, following the owner's square preference. The square was visually checked and preserved as PNG without cropping or resizing; the stable local cover URL is unchanged.
