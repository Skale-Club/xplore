# Grand Theft Auto 2 — original PC catalog notes

## Edition and progression

The selected edition is the original 1999 PC campaign. Downtown, Residential and Industrial have score gates of 1/3/5 million and church exits. Completing every gang contract is a separate optional route. The three Final Jobs trigger after all 21 gang jobs in their own district; they must not be made requirements of an ordinary score exit.

Final content has 16 categories, 107 bilingual tasks (29 story, 71 side, seven aggregate goals), 16 hidden Details and no invented native trophies. All 63 gang jobs are individually listed as side content, seven for each of nine gang/district combinations. Three Final Jobs and the opening tutorial explain the 66-versus-67 mission-count convention. Side lists and frozen keys preserve the chosen original-PC names, including Dimentia To Go! rather than the different PS1 title.

- [Version-specific Transporter Kill Frenzy save defect](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_2/Kill_Frenzies).

Root review distinguishes the PC hundred-target Mall objective and the PC Hot Dog Homicide bus sequence from altered PS1 versions. It also checks the eight Wang Cars, 150 Tokens, 60 Kill Frenzies, paid saves and score cushion. Failed gang jobs are retryable, unlike the permanent-within-run contract failure in the earlier engine. Complex guidance remains hidden in Details.

The save defect applies specifically to Transporter-carried challenge vehicles unloaded with a Truck Cab and a crane: on PC V9.6 or earlier, saving after activation can crash Resume Saved Status. The guidance was narrowed to those challenges. This is not a claim about every vehicle challenge or the unrelated 2026 Treasure Hunt mod.
