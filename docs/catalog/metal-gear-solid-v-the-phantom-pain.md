# Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain — PS4 catalog notes

## Edition and route

This entry covers The Phantom Pain on PlayStation 4, not Ground Zeroes or a separate native PS5 release. The numbered campaign operations are distinguished from Extreme, Subsistence and Total Stealth replays. Quiet's late route is optional for the main ending. Selected Side Ops and aggregate collection/base-development goals do not imply that every Side Op has its own detailed route.

Exact imported counts and validation results are recorded in the [sequential queue](https://app.notion.com/p/414b7a6861218101ae6ded91f74e1579). Longer unlock conditions and puzzle/quest solutions stay hidden behind Details.

## Online availability checked on 2026-11-09

[Konami support](https://eu-support.konami.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9669876317335-The-Phantom-Pain-Online) distinguishes PS4 availability from the PS3/Xbox 360 closure in May 2022. The [official service-status page](https://qr.konami.com/?lang=en) uses its public data/index.json feed: entry VF003-J1 is The Phantom Pain PS4, service_status 2, covering Japan/US/EU/Asia/Korea. The page's official js/lang.js maps status 2 to available and 3 to ended. PS4 Metal Gear Online is also listed available.

Intruder depends on online FOB infiltration. Do not claim that the platinum is wholly offline, or confuse online availability with the optional global nuclear-disarmament scene. An availability check is a dated observation, not a guarantee of future service.
