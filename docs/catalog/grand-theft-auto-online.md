# Grand Theft Auto Online — PS5 ongoing catalog notes

## Scope and shared trophies

This is a curated guide to selected permanent campaigns, heists, business cycles and collections, checked November 10, 2026. GTA Online has no single final ending or finite all-updates 100%. Finishing these checkboxes only completes the selected guide. The opening task and final review state this boundary in the app; do not hide it only in documentation.

The complete shared native PS5 trophy list is registered with GTA V. This entry deliberately has `trophies: []`, avoiding a second copy of the same PSN goals and platinum. It does not claim GTA Online lacks achievements. [Official standalone PS5 product](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP1004-PPSA03420_00-GTAOSTANDALONE01), [shared 78-trophy list](https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-v-psn/trophies/).

The selected route covers Casino Story, Dr. Dre's VIP Contract, Short Trips, Operation Paper Trail, First Dose/Last Dose, Cluckin' Bell Farm Raid, Project Overthrow and four FIB Files. Heist milestones cover the five original apartment chains, three Doomsday acts, one chosen Casino approach and one chosen Cayo Perico route. Named missions remain searchable. Basic business/rank goals and selected permanent collections supplement these chains without requiring every vehicle, property, weekly target or event. Lowriders, Oscar's complete chain and every other update are not all enumerated.

## Independently checked access rules

- Original apartment Fleeca uses two players; the other four original finales use four. Doomsday instanced setups/finales use two to four; Freemode preparations are a different stage. [Rockstar's Doomsday introduction](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak14o88389ooo3/GTA-Online-The-Doomsday-Heist-Out-Now).
- Casino Story missions support solo play following [official update 1.64](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/2CxAWnvruYhrsrUE3RvkYm/gtav-title-update-1-64-notes-ps5-ps4-xbox-series-x-s-xbox-one-pc); these are distinct from the Casino Heist's team finale. The same patch removed the HSW intro-race prerequisite for access to the upgrade service.
- Short Trips uses two players and no longer requires completing Dr. Dre's contract. [Rockstar's direct announcement](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/75733272kok74a/short-trips-with-franklin-and-lamar-now-available-for-all-players).
- Cayo Perico can be completed solo or with up to three others. Some loot access needs a second player; one solo finish is not a promise that every stash is accessible. [Official Cayo tips](https://www.rockstargames.com/reddeadonline/newswire/article/51974aa3aa1427/the-cayo-perico-heist-tips-and-tricks).
- Cluckin' Bell Farm Raid supports a complete solo run or a group of up to four. [Rockstar's raid page](https://www.rockstargames.com/gta-online?info=97535).

Access, team requirements and longer solutions are optional hidden Details. Do not repeat obsolete launch-era gates. [Update 1.72](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/0ExWSr9Bvq5Putzsn5w54/gtav-title-update-1-72-notes-ps5-ps4-xbox-one-pc-enhanced) makes Packie available by default for the Casino Heist and removes his former rescue event.
