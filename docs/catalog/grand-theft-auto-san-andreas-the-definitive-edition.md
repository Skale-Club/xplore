## Remaster and trophy audit

Supply Lines guidance does not treat the early PS2 release's continuous fuel depletion as the remaster rule. Plan five courier targets and the return to Zero's roof, using the actual fuel meter. Modern controls, autosaves and failed-mission retries do not establish a normal successful-mission replay menu; retain manual recovery saves.

Root independently checked the three mission-specific missables: Madd Dogg's Rhymes stealth, finding Mike Toreno directly at San Fierro's airport, and Black Project without a surface alarm. [DE roadmap](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-san-andreas-definitive-edition-trophy-guide-roadmap/). Suppressed weapons can assist stealth where the mission supplies them. A normal underground alarm is distinct from failing Black Project's surface condition; the alternate recovery entrance does not undo an alarm already triggered outside. Longer methods remain hidden Details.

Swiss Army Strife uses ten firearm skill categories; Tec-9/Micro SMG share one statistic. It does not require skill for every inventory item. Ain't Nothing But a G Thing adds all gang-war territories, property ownership and cash, beyond the native 35% territorial gate for the finale. Neither full territorial control nor maximum weapon skills are native 100% requirements.

Not a Player needs starting a date with each of the six girlfriends, without maximum relationships or a perfect date outcome. All fifty oysters remove appearance preferences and allow the four optional girlfriends to reappear, but do not revive the two story-linked girlfriends. Preserve Denise/Millie and record a date before choosing any card shortcut. [Firsthand DE girlfriend guide](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-san-andreas-definitive-all-girlfriends-locations/). Six individual searchable dating goals keep this separate from native completion.

The post-100% new-game trophy explicitly preserves the completed manual save; the guide does not recommend overwriting it. Historical crash reports are not treated as verified current-patch conditions.
