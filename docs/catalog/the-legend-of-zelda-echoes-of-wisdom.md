# The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom — Switch catalog notes

## Scope

The original Switch campaign is covered through Null, with the opening, Suthorn, regional rifts/dungeons, Hyrule Castle, the three goddess regions and Eternal Forest. Gerudo/Jabul and the later Eldin/Faron/Lanayru branches can be completed in different orders; the checklist presents one practical route.

The content file has 20 categories and 125 bilingual steps: 98 story, 21 selected side-content steps and six aggregate collection goals. Sixteen longer explanations remain optional spoilers behind Details. The optional section includes ten named Side Quests and six Dampé projects; it does not claim to enumerate every side quest or collectible location. There is no invented native Nintendo trophy list.

Verified collection totals: 127 echoes, 40 Pieces of Heart, 150 Might Crystals, 25 stamp stands and four Fairy Bottles. Bind is introduced inside Suthorn Ruins; the opening Still World traversal is not incorrectly described as a friend-collection repair. The final dungeon accounts for returning the Swordfighter equipment to Link and using echoes/Bind afterward.
