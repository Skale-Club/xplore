# Grand Theft Auto III – The Definitive Edition — PS5 catalog notes

## Separate remaster registration

This is the native PS5 remaster, kept separate from the original PS2 game. The [official individual product](https://store.playstation.com/en-gb/product/EP1004-PPSA03528_00-GTATHREE00000001) identifies the Definitive Edition and modern controls/targeting. The storefront's later listing date is not a claim that the remaster first launched then; the [native trophy reference](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-iii-the-definitive-edition/trophies/) records its November 2021 release.

The new source preserves the original entry's 125 task keys and relative order without changing that entry or its progress. It has nineteen categories, 139 bilingual tasks (64 story, 66 side and nine aggregate goals), 25 optional hidden Details and 29 native PS5 trophies: eleven bronze, eleven silver, six gold and one platinum. Fourteen extra trophy goals are searchable separately from the native completion route.

## Remaster and missability audit

The campaign/contact cutoffs and native 100% route remain relevant. Complete Luigi's and Joey's unfinished Portland jobs before Last Requests, Kenji's jobs before Waka-Gashira Wipeout, and the remaining early Asuka strand before Grand Theft Aero. The [Definitive Edition completion checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-3-definitive-edition-100-completion-guide-checklist/) independently supports the required route; save recovery is preferable to discovering missing contact missions after their cutoffs.

Retrying a failed mission is different from replaying a successfully completed mission. Do not promise a normal completed-mission replay menu. Keep separate manual saves before trophy-specific Portland jobs and later contact cutoffs. Claude still cannot swim; shoreline shortcuts must not assume the San Andreas movement rules. Original PS2 controls/patch reports are not presented as current-remaster instructions.

Root independently checked [the native PS5 list](https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-iii-the-definitive-edition-psn-2/trophies/) and [DE trophy roadmap](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-iii-the-definitive-edition/print-guide/). Four mission challenges are missable: Mike Lips' clean car, both Mafia companions alive, Chong's car-bomb trap, and using the Coach for all eight Fuzz Ball passengers. Extended methods remain hidden Details. [Car-bomb method](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-iii-the-definitive-edition/trophy/407192-planned-ahead.html).

Trophy-specific fifteen-fire/fifteen-criminal uninterrupted attempts are distinct from native per-island service totals. Liberty City Minute means surviving sixty seconds below ten health; it is not GTA IV's thirty-hour story trophy. Perfect Insane Stunt credit is distinct from the twenty designated Unique Jumps. Cheats are not a trophy-run method.
