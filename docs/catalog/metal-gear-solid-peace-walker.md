# Metal Gear Solid: Peace Walker — original PSP catalog notes

## Edition boundary

This is the original PlayStation Portable game. It includes the campaign continuation through Chapter 5 and the ZEKE conclusion; the first credits are not the endpoint. Selected Extra Ops and aggregate development/collection goals are separate from story progression. There are 33 Main Ops and 128 Extra Ops in the original edition; the checklist is a practical route rather than an individual detailed guide to all 128 extras.

PSP has no native PlayStation trophy list. Keep trophies empty; do not substitute PS3 HD or modern collection trophies. RetroAchievements remains a separate future provider topic. Multiplayer-related goals must distinguish original local/ad-hoc co-op from newer ports; the solo route does not require a new account connection.

## Artwork exception

Square artwork was sought first. The matching original PSP artwork available from [Konami's product page](https://www.konami.com/games/eu/en/products/mgspw/) is a 300 × 514 portrait packshot in its shop metadata: https://www.konami.com/products_master/eu_publish/mgspw/eu/en/images/en_mgspw_psp_standart.jpg . It is saved as PNG without resizing, cropping or stretching. The full artwork fits the standard frame with contain fitting. Do not substitute an HD Edition or Master Collection cover to obtain a square.

## Completion times

[HowLongToBeat #5919](https://howlongtobeat.com/game?id=5919) exactly matches the title and lists PlayStation Portable. Community estimates checked on 2026-11-09: 18h main, 28h main + extras, 92h completionist. These are whole-game estimates; the app does not infer precise personal remaining hours from equal-weight checkbox counts.
