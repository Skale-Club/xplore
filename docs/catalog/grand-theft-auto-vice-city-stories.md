# Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories — original PSP catalog notes

## Edition boundary

This registration uses the original 2006 PlayStation Portable game. Native PSP trophies are not invented. The [PSP-specific completion checklist](https://www.igrandtheftauto.com/gtavcs/guides/100-percent-completion-checklist-psp-version) covers 59 story missions, 99 red balloons, 30 rampages and 30 unique jumps. Large location sets and unnamed time-trial courses remain explicitly aggregate goals; named races and challenges have searchable titles.

The final source has 15 categories, 112 bilingual tasks (59 story / 45 side / eight aggregate goals), 16 optional hidden Details and no trophies.

The later PS2 version adds six unique jumps, five rampages and six activities: Rush!, Hyman Memorial O.D.T., Caddy Daddy, Crims on Water Wings, Playground on the Dock and Playground on the Park. These are excluded. Playground on the Town and Playground on the Point remain in the original PSP scope. The seven PSP checkpoint events plus Skywolf are grouped as eight challenge tasks, without importing the PS2-expanded twelve-event list.

Independent firsthand cross-platform reference: [DG-le-Ste walkthrough](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/938211-grand-theft-auto-vice-city-stories/faqs/64359), whose PS2 additions are explicitly marked. [Area GTA's checklist](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtavcs/100.html) includes the expanded PS2 content and must not be copied wholesale into a PSP entry.

## Story gates and empire completion

Blitzkrieg requires five owned empire sites; Blitzkrieg Strikes Again requires seven. Owning all thirty is full-completion work, not the requirement for those story transitions. [Blitzkrieg route](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-vice-city-stories/missions/blitzkrieg), [firsthand gate checks](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/938211-grand-theft-auto-vice-city-stories/faqs/45674).

Full empire work includes all thirty sites, a High Roller business of each of the six types, and completing the six job strands. It does not require upgrading all thirty properties to High Roller. Reputation targets are fifteen jobs each for Extortion, Loan Shark and Pimping, and six each for Drug Running, Smuggling and Robbery.

## Original service rules

Vigilante, Paramedic, Firefighter, Air Rescue and Beach Patrol use level-fifteen goals. Fire Copter has its own five-level goal. Checkpoint behavior differs from the older level-twelve games; guidance must not require restarting fifteen levels from the beginning after every failure.

Taxi completion has two cumulative alternatives: $5,000 earned or fifty fares. [Phillnanas's firsthand PSP guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/psp/933097-grand-theft-auto-vice-city-stories/faqs/61237) and the cross-platform walkthrough agree. The earnings path is usually quicker, but fifty completed fares must also be recognized. Controller instructions in ported guides still require platform checking.

Root independently checked [Air Rescue's four-person apparatus and Fire Copter's five levels](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtavcs/r3missions.html), and [Domestobot's Comms/lighter sequence and safe code](https://www.igrandtheftauto.com/gtavcs/guides/domo-arigato-domestoboto). The code is exposed only inside optional Details.

The optional Hostile Takeover guidance describes a reported percentage-credit defect, qualified as a report rather than a permanent completion block. [RARusk's firsthand route](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/938211-grand-theft-auto-vice-city-stories/faqs/48418) also explains the later Skywolf compensation. Root added that recovery context in both languages so an already completed story mission is not treated as a reason to restart the save.
