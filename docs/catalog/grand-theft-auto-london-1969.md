# Grand Theft Auto: London 1969 — PC mission-pack notes

## Edition and campaign

The original 1999 PC mission pack requires the original Grand Theft Auto installed. London 1961 is a separate later mission pack and is not merged into this entry. Four campaign chapters are covered: Boys Will Be Thieves has an introductory scooter job without a score gate; Mods and Sods requires 100,000 points, Chelsea Smile 200,000 and Dead Certainty 300,000, followed by their actual ending objectives.

Content has eight categories, 64 bilingual steps (43 story steps, 15 side, six aggregate goals) and 12 optional Details. Story-step counts include preparation and grouped objectives, not a claim of 43 mandatory individual jobs. Long instructions remain hidden behind Details.

## Mission-count convention and optional coverage

The operational PC checklist identifies 39 jobs: 1/13/12/13 across the chapters. Counting four ending sequences separately yields the alternate 43-entry convention. Promotional references to 32 missions are not treated as an exhaustive operational checklist. Completing every job is an optional aggregate; phone-stage locations are not all individually enumerated. The PC-only James Bomb vehicle job is excluded from the PS1 version. Optional Kill Frenzies total 21, seven in each score-based chapter and none in the introduction. The game has no official modern percentage meter or native trophy list.
