## Independent audit

The [PS4 native list](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/firewatch-na/trophies/) contains ten offline trophies: six bronze, one silver and three gold, with no platinum. Five original story trophies and five free Audio Tour update trophies share this one entry. NA/EU list variants do not require duplicate campaigns. The [updated PS4 roadmap](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/firewatch-na/guide/) distinguishes the initial missability notes from the patched Free Roam recovery: Bee Plot remains the story-dependent exception. Obtaining the optional goals during the campaign is a convenient route, not a claim that all five remain permanently missable.

Reviewed details include completing the disposable camera's remaining shots, explicitly keeping/adopting a turtle, opening the old cabin stove, keeping the hidden cassette after reaching its axe-gated route, and opening the Wapiti mailbox lid after entering the station on Day 77. Prying open the gate itself is not the Bee Plot trigger. Free Roam is unlocked by finishing the story; [Panic's PS4 support](https://help.panic.com/firewatch/ps4/freeroam-locked/) documents the End Game Save and Special Features menu. Do not promise Bee Plot recovery in Free Roam; an earlier save or story/Audio Tour replay provides a recovery route.

Independent story checks: [Day 33/conditional 34/64](https://www.gamepressure.com/firewatch/day-33-34-64/z28532), [Day 76](https://www.gamepressure.com/firewatch/day-76/z38533), [Day 77](https://www.gamepressure.com/firewatch/day-77/z48534), [Day 78](https://www.gamepressure.com/firewatch/day-78/z68536), [Day 79](https://www.gamepressure.com/firewatch/day-79/z78537). The optional Day 34 conversation depends on taking other lookouts' supplies and is not a mandatory chapter. The special radio-cache combination differs from ordinary supply caches. The cave route requires acquiring the later climbing equipment before the deeper descent. Dialogue choices are personal narrative choices, not a fabricated good-ending checklist.
