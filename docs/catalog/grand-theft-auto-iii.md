# Grand Theft Auto III — original PS2 catalog notes

## Edition and scope

This is the original PlayStation 2 release, with 18 categories, 125 bilingual tasks (64 story, 52 side and nine aggregate goals), 18 optional hidden Details and no native trophies. The later PS4 conversion and Definitive Edition trophy lists do not belong to the original PS2 game.

All 17 phone jobs and eight off-road/RC activities are individually named. The common 73-mission convention combines 48 story/contact missions, 17 phone jobs, four off-road events and four RC challenges; checklist steps also include preparation, transitions and aggregate completion goals, so 125 tasks does not mean 125 missions.

## Route and optional guidance audit

The ten permanently missable contact jobs are separated as optional completionist content: two Luigi, two Joey, five Kenji and one Asuka. Finish the early Portland contact work before Last Requests, all five Kenji jobs before Waka-Gashira Wipeout!, and Two-Faced Tanner before Grand Theft Aero. Increased Mafia hostility after Sayonara Salvatore makes early Portland extras harder; it is distinct from contact lockouts. Marked Man follows both Plaster Blaster and A Drop in the Ocean.

Root independently checked Espresso-2-Go!'s nine stalls and timer trigger, The Exchange's $500,000 entrance requirement and confiscated weapons, S.A.M.'s supplied launcher/shore access/cargo recovery, Marty's hidden phone availability, service rules and import lists. Detailed solutions remain behind explicit Details clicks. GTA III has lethal water: S.A.M. guidance explicitly directs the player onto dry land before aiming.

Taxi fares are 100 cumulative; Paramedic requires level 12 in one uninterrupted attempt. Firefighter and Vigilante require 20 completions per island. Imports comprise 16 Portland deliveries, 16 Shoreside deliveries and seven emergency-crane deliveries, not 39 unique vehicle models. Aggregate goals cover 100 hidden packages, 20 rampages and 20 unique jumps.
