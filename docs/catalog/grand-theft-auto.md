# Grand Theft Auto — original PC catalog notes

## Edition and progression

The selected edition is the 1997 PC campaign. It has six score-and-exit chapters: Gangsta Bang (1,000,000), Heist Almighty (2,000,000), Mandarin Mayhem (2,000,000), Tequila Slammer (3,000,000), Bent Cop Blues (3,000,000) and Rasta Blasta (5,000,000). The game permits flexible work selection toward each score gate; completing every optional contract is not mandatory campaign progression.

Content has 11 categories and 88 bilingual tasks: 64 story, 18 side and six aggregate mastery goals, with 14 hidden Details. The 90 PC mission segments include introductions, phone continuations and vehicle jobs, not 90 payphones. The PS1 version omits two train-related contracts. Seventy Kill Frenzies and an all-jobs ledger are optional aggregates, not an invented native 100% statistic or exhaustive per-location guide. No native Steam/PSN trophies are fabricated.

Long instructions cover frame limiting, pager/phone messages, score multipliers, lives and chapter restarts, hospital/arrest differences, later phone groups, special vehicle objectives and the final exit. Their spoiler content remains hidden behind Details.
