# Red Dead Redemption — native PlayStation 5 catalog notes

## Selected edition

Use the native PS5 edition released on 2025-12-02. Rockstar confirms that it includes the complete single-player Red Dead Redemption and Undead Nightmare experiences and Game of the Year bonus content. The normal campaign and bundled Undead Nightmare have separate routes, saves, completion counters and optional goals. Older PS3 multiplayer trophies do not belong to this release.

The main campaign route includes New Austin, Mexico, West Elizabeth, the ranch return and the post-story Remember My Family conclusion. Selected Stranger chains and aggregate completion goals remain separate from the story route. Long dueling, treasure, challenge and missable guidance is revealed only through optional Details.

## Native trophies

The PS5 list has 52 trophies: 44 bronze, four silver, three gold and one platinum. Unlike the walkthrough's optional expansion route, the native trophy list includes Undead Nightmare requirements for its platinum. Do not describe completing only the normal campaign as earning platinum.

An independent audit compares all 52 native names from Exophase to the guide's full individual-tier table. Non-bronze trophies are Legend of the West (platinum), Nurture or Nature?, Redeemed and Zed's Dead, Baby (gold), and People are Still Strange, Frontiersman, All's Right With the World and Spinning Plates (silver). The remaining 44 are bronze. Spurred to Victory needs advance attention to its mount condition; mission replay is not a blanket cure for every missable requirement.

## Undead completion source conflict

The brief Undead subsection of a general trophy guide conflicts with the detailed firsthand completion checklist. It omits Missing Persons and claims all eight outfits are necessary. The checklist instead identifies eight story missions, six Survivor side missions, five cemeteries, 15 Missing Persons, the Undead Hunter outfit and four rank-five Undead challenge chains, alongside rescued towns. Outfit collecting is a separate optional goal. Use the in-game completion ledger and actual saved-town markers for final checks; the all-saved-at-once trophy is separate from first-time rescue credit. Do not import the contradictory all-outfits requirement.
