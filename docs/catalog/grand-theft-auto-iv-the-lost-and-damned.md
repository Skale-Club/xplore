# Grand Theft Auto IV: The Lost and Damned — PS3 catalog notes

## Edition boundary

This is the original PlayStation 3 expansion, with its own Johnny Klebitz campaign and completion statistic. Do not duplicate it as an Episodes from Liberty City campaign or copy the Complete Edition's combined time estimate. Original PS3 trophies remain the expansion's five bronze entries in GTA IV's shared list; there is no separate platinum and no copied base-game trophy list. [Full PS3 trophy groups](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-iv/trophies/).

## Route and completion checks

Root independently checked the [original mission index](https://gta.rdy.jp/gta4tlad/missions.html), [Area GTA completion rules](https://gta.rdy.jp/gta4tlad/100.html) and [Psy's checklist](https://www.igrandtheftauto.com/gtatlad/guides/100-percent-completion-checklist). Native completion requires the 22-story route, ten Angus thefts, five Stubbs jobs, twelve distinct race wins, twenty-five Gang Wars, fifty seagulls, Malc's two encounters and Dave's encounter, the four game victories and seven friendship activities. Brian's conditional encounter is excluded.

Do not require seven friendship activities twice. The original reference permits one completed outing per activity with an available friend; the archive checklist explicitly uses Clay **or** Terry. Winning the four games is a separate requirement that can overlap with an outing. Full Chat instead needs both friends' toughness at 100%; that is a trophy goal, not the native friendship-activity percentage. Fifty Gang Wars is an optional weapon-reward milestone; twenty-five is sufficient for native completion.

Get Good Wood counts 69 cumulative bat knockoffs during motorcycle races. It is not a single-race or 69-race requirement. [PS3 trophy guide](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-iv/guide/). The five DLC trophies have single-player requirements; no current multiplayer availability is asserted. Hi-Lo is not needed for native completion, and the original clubhouse version becomes unavailable after the story. [Original mini-game reference](https://gta.rdy.jp/gta4tlad/minigames.html).

The final draft contains twelve categories, 69 bilingual tasks (22 story, 45 side and two aggregate completion goals), thirteen optional hidden Details and five bronze trophies. Named thefts, races, contracts and encounters are searchable; large unnamed collections and repeated Gang Wars are explicitly aggregate goals. Root independently checked the PS3 bat buttons, cumulative knockoffs, the passenger-shooting and pipe-bomb sequence in Shifting Weight, and free weapons before Get Lost. Final guidance follows the crew to the clubhouse marker without adding an unsupported mandatory police-escape stage.
