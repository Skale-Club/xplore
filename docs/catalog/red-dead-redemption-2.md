# Red Dead Redemption 2 — PlayStation 4 catalog notes

## Edition and route

The selected edition is PS4 Standard, SKU UP1004-CUSA03041_00-REDEMPTIONFULL02. The official Store listing includes both Story Mode and Red Dead Online. The campaign covers all six chapters and both epilogues through American Venom. Optional honor missions and selected Stranger chains are distinguished from mandatory story progression; Special/Ultimate bonus missions are not silently made requirements of Standard Edition.

Source mission totals vary with split mission parts, optional honor threads, updates and bonus content. Verify the actual named route and prerequisites rather than equating an unqualified web total with mandatory missions. Long solutions, missable choices and post-story grave locations remain behind optional Details.

## Native trophies and missables

The PS4 list contains 52 trophies: 44 bronze, four silver, three gold and one platinum. There are 34 offline trophies and 17 directly associated with Red Dead Online, plus the platinum requiring both modes. Online objectives must be labeled in English and Portuguese. Completion-time estimates for Story Mode are not a promise of platinum time.

Before leaving the relevant camps, review Lending a Hand, Friends With Benefits, Give to the Poor and Errand Boy. The in-game 100% ledger, full animal trophy coverage and optional compendium collection have different requirements. Do not turn every Guarma-exclusive animal or optional species into a compulsory trophy missable.
