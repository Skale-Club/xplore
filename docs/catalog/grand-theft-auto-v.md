# Grand Theft Auto V — native PS5 catalog notes

## Edition and route

This registration uses the native PlayStation 5 release, including its current 78-trophy PSN list. It does not use the smaller original PS3 list or create a second game for the PS4/PS5 purchase bundle. [Official PlayStation product](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP1004-PPSA03420_00-GTAVCROSSGENBUND/), [PS5 trophy listing](https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-v-psn/trophies/).

Only the chosen heist approach and one ending are required on a save. Branch-specific preparations must not become a demand to finish incompatible approaches together. The in-game 69-story counter is not a literal count of every alternative preparation represented by editorial app tasks. Four optional Lester assassinations may be saved until after the finale; the Hotel Assassination is part of story progression.

Longer advice is optional hidden Details. Keep a manual save before the final choice. Option C preserves access to the three protagonists and their optional trophy work, but it is not the only ending that counts for native completion. [Three Man Army and character-specific missability](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-5-ps4/guide/).

## Native completion versus broader mastery

The game's official checklist uses 69 story credits, twenty Strangers and Freaks credits, 42 hobbies/pastimes, fourteen random events and sixteen miscellaneous goals. The [completion reference](https://www.gtabase.com/grand-theft-auto-v/guides/gta-5-100-completion-guide-checklist) explains that Franklin has 24 relevant mission stages despite twenty checklist credits. Michael/Trevor side strands, later post-100% missions and enhanced-edition extras remain distinguishable from the native minimum.

Medals rather than universal golds suffice for many hobbies. Native collectible minima include 25 of fifty stunt jumps, 25 of fifty bridge flights and eight of fifteen knife flights. The corresponding broader trophy goals are separate. Named activities remain searchable; large location collections use explicitly aggregate goals. The game statistic, not a checkbox-count percentage, is the final native-completion test.

[Solid Gold, Baby!](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-5/trophy/68555-solid-gold-baby-.html) needs seventy eligible mission medals; hobby medals do not substitute. Completed criteria can combine across valid replays except the five Rampages, which require all criteria in one run. Partial progress toward one criterion is not accumulated into a successful quota. Stock guidance avoids guaranteed return percentages and distinguishes LCN from network-dependent BAWSAQ.

## Shared trophies and GTA Online

The full PS5 list has 59 bronze, fifteen silver, three gold and one platinum: base 51 (42/5/3/1), Heists nine (7/2/0/0), Rockstar Editor ten (7/3/0/0), and Doomsday eight (3/5/0/0). The base platinum includes seventeen direct GTA Online trophies; DLC trophies are not platinum prerequisites. [Full distribution and current descriptions](https://www.truetrophies.com/game/grand-theft-auto-v/trophies), [PS5-native list](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-5-ps5/trophies/).

Keep the complete shared trophy set in this GTA V entry. The separate GTA Online catalog entry is a scoped ongoing milestone guide and intentionally does not duplicate those same PSN records. Online requirements are labeled; this registration does not connect a player account or promise every mode's current matchmaking availability.

Current PS5 Vinewood Visionary and Majestic descriptions include their Director Mode entry alternatives, rather than only the older video-export wording. Do not substitute Xbox points for PlayStation trophy tiers or accept a guide's tier classification without checking it.
