# The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening — Switch 2019 catalog notes

## Edition boundary

This is the 2019 Nintendo Switch remake, stored under a year-qualified slug while retaining the official display title. It is separate from the original Game Boy game and Link's Awakening DX. The route includes all eight instrument dungeons, required village/trade/Marin/ghost/rooster progression and the Wind Fish's Egg finale. Color Dungeon, Chamber Dungeons and selected minigames/upgrades are optional sections.

Use the remake's counts and mechanics: 50 Secret Seashells, 32 Pieces of Heart and three Fairy Bottles. Dampé's Ticking Clock challenge provides a separate full Heart Container; the 32 pieces do not include that container. The trade sequence has fourteen items before the optional Boomerang exchange. Amiibo-only content is not required for the ordinary campaign route or standard collection goals.
