# Grand Theft Auto: Chinatown Wars — Nintendo DS catalog notes

## Edition and offline scope

This registration uses the original Nintendo DS game. [Nintendo's original product page](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-DS/Grand-Theft-Auto-Chinatown-Wars-270913.html) identifies the system, Rockstar publisher and March 2009 release. Its notice confirms Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection ended in May 2014. Do not add the later PSP-only Melanie missions or PSP-only time trials to the DS completion route.

The 58-mission main campaign is distinct from the two Xin bonus missions. Those bonus unlocks historically required Social Club synchronization; they are not required for native 100% and are excluded from the registered route. Finding the two post-story Lions of Fo is a separate collectible requirement, not the same action as downloading Xin missions. The original reference describes collecting the statues first and synchronizing afterward. [Original DS completion requirements](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtacw/100.html), [historical Lions/Xin sequence](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtacw/lionsoffo.html).

Nintendo DS has no native platform trophy list. The eight physical safehouse ornaments are in-game collection goals; do not fabricate PSN/Steam trophy records for them.

## Completion and platform mechanics

Root independently checked the original DS references. Bronze is sufficient for the service jobs, applicable odd jobs, every required race/time-trial course, thirty Rampages and five Riding Shotgun challenges. Gold rewards are additional mastery, not mandatory native completion. Named courses and activities remain searchable; large location sets use explicitly aggregate goals. [Odd jobs and course lists](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtacw/odd.html).

The four service jobs have their own rules. Taxi bronze is five consecutive fares, not the hundred-fare totals from earlier games. DS Paramedic transports one patient at a time and uses touchscreen revival; older three-patient or unlimited-capacity instructions do not apply. [Firsthand DS service reference](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtacw/r3missions.html).

All eight random-character strands need their available follow-ups; Wilhelm's third meeting awards a safehouse. [Original encounter guide](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtacw/randomchar.html). Dealer discovery means opening each dealer's trade interface, without necessarily purchasing. The 21 safehouses include fourteen purchases and seven awards; the checklist must not demand 21 purchases. Camera and jump goals cover 100 cameras and thirty successful unique jumps.

The eight ornaments include trade participation, dealer-discovery, profit/loss and property awards. Conflicting online guide quantities must not inflate native goals; source-specific criteria and difficult sequences belong only in optional hidden Details. App checkboxes measure the curated guide, while the game's own percentage remains the final native-completion check.
