# Grand Theft Auto IV: The Ballad of Gay Tony — PS3 catalog notes

## Edition and separate completion systems

This registration uses the original PlayStation 3 expansion starring Luis Lopez. Episodes from Liberty City is a bundle, not another duplicate campaign. Only the ten native bronze TBoGT trophies belong here; the base 51 and Lost and Damned's five trophies remain in their own entries. There is no separate expansion platinum. [Shared PS3 trophy list](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-iv/trophies/).

The full campaign has 26 mission tasks. [Area GTA's original completion reference](https://gta.rdy.jp/gta4tbogt/100.html) explains why some checklists say 25: the first Blog This! stage does not independently increase the native completion percentage. Both parts remain searchable and distinct. Native game completion is different from earning 100% scores in every mission.

[Gold Star's PS3 guide](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/grand-theft-auto-iv/trophy/14896-tbogt-gold-star.html) confirms criteria can accumulate over separate mission replays. Do not require a single run combining every headshot, speed and damage target. Phone replays unlock after finishing the story; cheats, taxis and restarting a failed attempt can invalidate score recording. The guide keeps this explanation inside optional hidden Details. Past the Velvet Rope checks at least 80% in every mission; Gold Star checks 100% in every mission. Neither replaces the native completion statistic.

## Side content

Root independently checked [the completion checklist](https://www.gtabase.com/grand-theft-auto-iv/gta-iv-the-ballad-of-gay-tony-100-completion-guide-checklist): eight club VIP errands, all three random-character strands, twenty-five Drug Wars, three triathlons, fifteen base jumps, fifty seagulls, required games/dancing and seven friend activities. Higher Drug Wars milestones are optional rewards.

Friend activities need one outing per type with Henrique or Armando, not two duplicate sets. Game victories remain distinct from simply participating during outings. [Psy's original-activity breakdown](https://www.igrandtheftauto.com/gtatbogt/guides/100-percent-completion-checklist). [The VIP errand reference](https://www.gtabase.com/grand-theft-auto-iv/side-missions/club-management) distinguishes the eight errands from ordinary repeating nightclub security events. Named errands, jumps and missions are searchable; large unnamed sets are explicitly aggregate goals.
