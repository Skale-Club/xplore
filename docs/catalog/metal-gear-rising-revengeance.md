# Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance — PS3 catalog notes

## Edition boundary

The original PS3 campaign covers Files R-00 through R-07. Jetstream Sam and Blade Wolf are optional installed DLC stories; VR expansion content is separate from the base VR missions. Longer combat solutions, boss-phase guidance and difficult unlock conditions stay hidden behind Details.

The native PS3 trophy list has 61 entries: 51 base-game trophies plus 10 optional DLC trophies (two VR expansion, four Sam, four Wolf). Base distribution: 42 bronze, five silver, three gold, one platinum. Overall: 46 bronze, eight silver, six gold, one platinum. The base platinum must not require the ten DLC trophies. Label DLC requirements clearly within the existing flat trophy schema; do not invent a new database grouping field.

## Artwork exception

Square artwork was sought first. The available official Steam app icon is only 32 × 32 and is unsuitable as a readable catalog cover. Use Konami's matching full PS3 packshot instead: https://www.konami.com/products_master/eu_publish/mgr/eu/en/images/en_mgr_ps3_standart.jpg . It is 300 × 346, visually checked and encoded as PNG without cropping, stretching or resizing. The entire original artwork fits the standard frame with contain fitting.
