# Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots

Added on 2026-11-09 for the original PlayStation 3 edition.

- 18 categories and 108 bilingual tasks: 97 story, six selected personal
  challenges and five aggregate collection goals.
- All five acts, the ending and compatible clear-data saving are covered.
- Eight optional EN/PT explanations cover Octopus, Naomi's trail, the resistance
  tail, Wolf, Vamp, REX/RAY, Mantis and the final corridor. Puzzle solutions remain
  hidden behind Details; short instructions avoid revealing those solutions.
- The final PS3 trophy list has 34 entries: 19 bronze, nine silver, five gold and
  one platinum. Trophy support requires update 2.00 or an updated retail release.
  The preliminary 2012 announcement differs from the shipped list and is not the
  authoritative source for every requirement or tier.
- No Metal Gear Online, provider account linking or modern Master Collection
  trophy list is included. Aggregate goals are not exhaustive location guides.

The cover is the existing square 1024 × 1024 MGS4 artwork hosted by IGN. Its JPEG
was encoded as PNG without resizing, cropping or changing the artwork:
https://assets-prd.ignimgs.com/2022/01/08/metal-gear-solid-4-guns-of-the-patriots-button-ve-1641602845318.jpg

## Verification

JSON, translations/fallbacks, keys, category kinds, details, cover dimensions and
all final trophy names/tiers passed checks. Official preview/apply and independent
read-back matched the new game. A repeat preview reported zero inserts or orphan
rows. All eight prior games, 129 categories, 919 tasks and 281 trophy rows were
independently compared and unchanged. No pruning, push, deployment or production
change occurred. Cover publication remains part of the pending release.

An initial live HowLongToBeat lookup exposed the old client's obsolete endpoint
(HTTP 404). The catalog-wide follow-up repaired the client and verified source #5916:
18h Main Story, 21h Main + Extra and 38h Completionist community estimates,
now cached in the source file and development database.
