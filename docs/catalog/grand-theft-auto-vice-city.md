# Grand Theft Auto: Vice City — original PS2 catalog notes

## Edition and route

This registration uses the original 2002 PlayStation 2 game. The original PS2 has no native trophy list; PS4 conversion and Definitive Edition trophies belong to their respective later editions.

The final source has 17 categories, 126 bilingual steps (38 story / 79 side / nine aggregate completion goals), 18 optional hidden Details and no trophies. Campaign, asset missions, optional contact strands, all five phone contracts and named competitions are covered. Preparation and completion milestones are checklist steps, not a claim of 126 separate missions.

The route distinguishes completing the story from completing every optional business and side activity. The finale gate requires Cop Land for the Vercetti Estate, Print Works through Hit the Courier, and five other purchased assets generating income. That means six purchased businesses including Print Works, plus the Estate. Phil's Place does not count. Sunshine Autos starts generating revenue after its first delivery list; all four lists and all six street races belong to full completion, not the minimum story gate.

Root cross-checked [original mission routing](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtavc/missions.html), [asset missions](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtavc/amissions.html), [Sunshine Autos](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtavc/amissions5.html), [finale walkthrough](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtavc/missions6.html) and [Cap the Collector's original-game walkthrough](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-vice-city/missions/cap-the-collector).

## Original-version details and completion

Demolition Man's original timer is seven minutes, starting with the first bomb pickup. Some current guides describe the Definitive Edition's longer timer and different controls. Original firsthand PS2 references: [Minesweeper_, 2003](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/561545-grand-theft-auto-vice-city/faqs/20417), [A_K_A](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/561545-grand-theft-auto-vice-city/faqs/19994). Death Row requires reaching Lance before his health expires, then completing the hospital escape; optional preparation should happen before starting.

Checkpoint Charlie has 26 checkpoints and an initial 2:30 timer in the original game; its first successful clear satisfies the asset/completion requirement. The later mobile/Definitive three-minute allowance must not be substituted. Pole Position's condition is $300 spent in the private room, rather than an unreliable fixed waiting-time claim. Sources: [original PS2 guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/561545-grand-theft-auto-vice-city/faqs/34325), [Psy's original guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps2/561545-grand-theft-auto-vice-city/faqs/20948).

Original PS2 Cherry Popper saving has a reported corruption defect. Optional guidance recommends another save location, without claiming that all later versions share the bug. [Firsthand original-version FAQ](https://www.neoseeker.com/gta-vicecity/faqs/1234109-grand-theft-auto-vice-city-n.html).

Service requirements are Pizza Boy level 10, 100 cumulative taxi fares, and level 12 for Paramedic, Firefighter and Vigilante. Hunter's Brown Thunder can satisfy Vigilante. Rifle Range requires 45 points for full completion, beyond a basic 30-point pass; all purchasable safehouses also matter. [Original service guide](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtavc/r3missions.html), [100% checklist](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtavc/100.html), [original activity list](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtavc/omissions.html).
