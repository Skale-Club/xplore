# Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories — original PSP catalog notes

## Edition and scope

This registration selects the original 2005 PlayStation Portable release. It has no native trophies. PSP multiplayer is separate from the single-player completion route and must not become an invented 100% requirement. Later PS2/mobile controls must not be substituted for the PSP's controls.

The final source has 13 categories, 111 bilingual tasks (70 story / 34 side / seven aggregate goals), 16 optional hidden Details and no trophies. Root added the optional Panlantic document-preservation explanation and moved the equipment advance before Toshiko's jobs, so the follow-up-call wait can be used productively. Stable keys were preserved.

The main route covers 70 named missions through The Sicilian Gambit. Large location collections remain explicit aggregate goals, while named races and activities have searchable task titles. Optional game-completion work is separate from reaching the story ending.

References: [original mission index](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtalcs/missions.html), [PSP firsthand 100% walkthrough by btaylorstl, 2006](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/psp/925776-grand-theft-auto-liberty-city-stories/faqs/43655).

## Completion mechanics audit

[Original completion checklist](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtalcs/100.html): 100 hidden packages, 20 rampages, 26 unique jumps and 16 Love Media deliveries; three level-12 emergency/law-enforcement services and 100 cumulative taxi fares. Avenging Angels and Trash Dash require separate island clears. Bumps & Grinds requires all ten courses, alongside three other checkpoint activities and six named street races. Native percentage flags are not the app's checkbox count.

[Slash TV](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtalcs/slashtv.html) needs two complete five-round runs; the Cox Mascot outfit verifies the second completion. Its initial access requires the overalls, completed Portland Chainsaw Masquerade and a night visit. The PSP firsthand guide independently confirms these conditions.

[Tandem activity rules](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtalcs/tandem.html): 9mm Mayhem requires more than ten targets, Scooter Shooter more than eight, and AWOL Angel more than ten. The PSP walkthrough's actual Scooter section also says nine targets; a contradictory ten-target value in its closing summary is not used. Guide labels such as level 11 must not obscure the player's destruction counter.

Area GTA sometimes displays PS2 controller instructions. Its mission/activity mechanics are cross-checked against the PSP guide; instructions referring to a right analog stick are not used for the selected PSP platform.

## Independent route review

[Salvatore's final strand](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtalcs/missions3_1.html) checks Dead Reckoning's shore interception, the Shogun Showdown disguise/tank disposal, Shoreside Redemption's convoy protection and Sicilian Gambit's driving, boat-gun and lighthouse phases. [Donald's later strand](https://gta.rdy.jp/gtalcs/missions3_2.html) distinguishes preserving the plans in Panlantic Land Grab from destroying the vehicle, the $20,000 preparation payment, and Bringing the House Down's placement and alternate-exit sequence. Detailed spoilers belong only in optional Details.
