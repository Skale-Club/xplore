# The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes — Nintendo 3DS catalog notes

## Edition and service boundary

This is the 2015 Nintendo 3DS mainline entry with its free content updates. The main route covers the original eight areas and 32 levels through Lady Maud and the ending. Solo play uses three controllable heroes via Doppels; local cooperative play requires three participants. Ordinary Internet matchmaking is not a current goal: Nintendo ended 3DS online functionality on 2024-04-08. Local and other offline modes remain available.

The three additional Drablands Challenges per original level are optional, giving 96 extra challenge clears beyond the 32 ordinary clears. The updated Den of Trials is a separate optional gauntlet: eight zones, five floors each, including safe rooms. Its 40 floors are not 40 additional main campaign levels. Update 2.0 lets the Street Merchant provide Friendly Tokens after lifting Styla's curse; they must not be described as permanently requiring local friends. Update 2.1 adds hero-point and material conveniences. Outfit guidance identifies the installed-update and story conditions.

The final draft has 13 categories, 104 steps (80 story, 18 side, six aggregate goals) and 14 bilingual Details. Root reviewed all 32 named levels and branch prerequisites, then corrected the opening's Sir Combsly/silk/tunic sequence and the Lady's Pets and Blizzagia instructions. Long puzzle, item-height and solo-switching guidance stays hidden behind optional Details. Native Nintendo trophies are not invented.
