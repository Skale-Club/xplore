# Metal Gear Solid V: Ground Zeroes — updated PS4 catalog notes

## Edition and scope

This entry covers the updated PS4 game: the Ground Zeroes story mission, four Side Ops, and both Déjà Vu and Jamais Vu Extra Ops. Jamais Vu was added to PlayStation by the free May 2014 update; its launch-time Xbox exclusivity does not describe the updated edition. Side/extra missions are separate from the main story route.

The native PS4 list has 15 trophies: 13 bronze, one silver (Skilled), one gold (Hero), and no platinum. The original trophy requirements and later-added mission content must be distinguished. Long rescue routes, bonus-objective conditions and collection solutions remain hidden behind optional Details.
