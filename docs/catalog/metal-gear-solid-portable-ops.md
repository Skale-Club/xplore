# Metal Gear Solid: Portable Ops — original PSP catalog notes

## Edition and route

This entry covers the original 2006 PSP campaign, not Portable Ops Plus. The route runs from prison escape and squad recruitment through the medical/intelligence leads, rail-bridge diversions, harbor/warhead route, Python, first Null encounter, Snake rescue, RAXA, the power-substation route, second Null encounter, silo, Cunningham and Gene. Do not invent a universal mission numbering scheme for its area-based progression.

Recruitment uses dragging/extraction through the truck and squad system; Peace Walker's Fulton mechanics must not be copied into this game. Optional recruit chains and support/squad goals are separate from campaign completion. PSP has no native PlayStation trophies: keep trophies empty, with RetroAchievements a future provider topic. Do not require obsolete internet matchmaking for the story route.

## Artwork exception

A suitable square original-edition cover was sought first. The preserved original PSP front cover is 300 × 517, from the [Push Square game listing](https://www.pushsquare.com/games/psp/metal_gear_solid_portable_ops): https://images.pushsquare.com/14a2a61db8252/metal-gear-solid-portable-ops-cover.cover_300x.jpg . The complete portrait artwork is encoded as PNG without resizing, cropping or stretching and fits the app's standard frame with contain fitting. The publisher product page supplies a logo and screenshots, not a matching square cover.
