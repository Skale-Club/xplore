# Grand Theft Auto: London 1961 — PC mission-pack notes

## Edition and campaign boundary

The free 1999 second PC mission pack requires the original GTA and London 1969. It is a prequel set in 1961, not another 1969 chapter or a standalone PS1 release. Its only single-player chapter, When We Were Very Small, ends after the 100,000-point goal and Harold's meeting. Manchester is an optional network-deathmatch map, not another campaign city or a modern Internet-matching promise.

Content: seven categories, 42 tasks (16 story, 21 side, five aggregate goals), ten hidden bilingual Details and no invented native trophies. Four phone contracts, one radio assignment and two vehicle jobs make seven playable jobs; the ending supplies an eighth entry in lists that count it separately. Completing all seven jobs and all seven Kill Frenzies is an optional community full clear, not a prerequisite for the campaign ending or a native percentage meter. Vehicle totals in retrospective descriptions are not mistaken for mission totals.
